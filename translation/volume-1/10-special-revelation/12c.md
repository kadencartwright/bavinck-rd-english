Nevertheless, revelation forms an order of things distinct from the ordinary _ordo naturae_: a system of words and deeds of God that is itself governed by one principle and appears before us as an organic whole. Revelation is no _einzelner Akt Gottes in der Zeit_, isolated from the whole of nature, Strauss, _Glaub._ I 274, but is a world in itself, distinct from nature and yet adapted to it, related to it, and intended for it.

In this system of revelation, which begins at paradise and ends only in the parousia, much is still obscure and unexplained. Main lines can be drawn. In the history of both prophecies and miracles, order and progression can be discovered. Revelation too has its own laws and rules. It is the beautiful task of the _historia revelationis_ to trace these and discover the system hidden in its history. Yet there are still many facts whose proper significance for and connection with the whole are not yet understood; still many words and deeds that cannot be brought under any rule. This should cause no surprise and may least of all be exploited as a ground for unbelief. The philosophy of nature and history too is far from finished with its labor. It too confronts at every moment _cruces_ that it does not know how to explain. Yet no one therefore doubts the unity of nature and the plan of history.

Compared with these, revelation even stands in a favorable position. Its main lines are fixed. As it begins in paradise and ends in the parousia, it forms a grand history that sheds light upon the whole of nature and history and therein, according to Augustine’s word, keeps the extraordinary from excess through the ordinary and ennobles the ordinary through the extraordinary, in Müller, _Natur und Wunder_, 180. Without it we walk in darkness, go toward the eternal rest of death, and have no answer to the question why everything has existed. But with it we find ourselves in a world that, despite every sinful power, is being led toward restoration and perfection. Israel the preparation, Christ the center, the church the working out, the parousia the crown—that is the thread that connects the facts of revelation with one another.
