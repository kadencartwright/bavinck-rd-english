> [1] For brevity, a few works are listed here on the councils, Church Fathers, ecclesiastical writers, etc., and the literature concerning them. The acts of the councils are collected in: Joa. Harduinus, *Conciliorum collectio regia maxima.* Par. 1714. 11 Tomi in 12 vol., extending through 1714. J. Domin. Mansi. *Sacrorum conciliorum nova et amplissima collectio.* Flor. 1759-1788. 31 vol.; a new, improved edition has been appearing in Paris since 1885. *Acta et decreta sacrorum conciliorum recentiorum. Collectio Lacensis* (Maria Laach), 7 vol. Frib. Brisg. 1870-1890, contains the *acta* of the *concilia* from 1682-1870. The principal decisions of councils and popes were collected by H. Denzinger. *Enchiridion symbolorum et definitionum, ed. 6 aucta ab Ign. Stahl.* Wirceb. 1888. The history of the councils is treated most fully by Karl Joseph von Hefele, *Conciliëngeschichte, nach den Quellen bearbeitet*, 7 Bde. Freiburg 1855–1874, 2e Aufl. Bd. 1-4 by Hefele himself, Bd. 5-6 by Knöpfler; the work was continued by Hergenröther, who added Bd. 8-9 to it in 1887 and 1890.
>
> The Church Fathers and other ecclesiastical writers were published, among others, by: Andr. Gallandius, *Bibliotheca veterum patrum antiquorumque scriptorum ecclesiasticorum*, Venet. 1763-1781, 14 tomi fol. J. P. Migne, *Patrologiae cursus completus, sive Bibliotheca universatis .... omnium S. S. Patrum, doctorum scriptorumque ecclesiasticorum, sive latinorum, sive graecorum.* Paris 1844 sq. 473 tomi; series latina 221 tomi, series graeca 166 tomi, patrologia graeca latine tantum edita 86 tomi. Furthermore, various Church Fathers were published separately by the Mauriner monks (Cf. art. Herz²), such as Origenes by Delarue Paris 1733, Chrysostomus by Montfaucon 1718, Augustinus by Delfau et al. 1679-1702, etc. Finally, an excellent edition of the Latin Church Fathers is appearing in Vienna under the direction of the Imperial Academy of Sciences: *Corpus scriptorum ecclesiasticorum Latinorum.* Vindob. 1866 sq., in which various Church Fathers—Minucius Felix, Cyprianus, Arnob., Lact., etc.—or portions of Church Fathers—Tert., August., etc.—have already appeared. See further Bellarminus, *De Scriptoribus ecclesiasticis* Romae 1613. Ed. du Pin, *Nouvelle Bibliothèque des auteurs ecclésiastiques*, 47 vol. Par. 1686-1714. Cave, *Historia literaria script. eccles.* 2 vol. Lond. 1689, Basil. 1741. Walch, *Bibl. theol. sel.* Jenae 4 vol. 1757 sq. Ritter, *Geschichte der christl. Philosophie*, 4 Theile, Hamburg 1841-1845. Ueberweg, *Grundriss der Gesch. d. Phil. der patr. u. schol. Zeit.* 6e Aufl. 1881. Nirschl. *Lehrb. der Patrol. u. Patristik*, 3 Bde. Mainz 1881-1885. Alzog, *Grundriss der Patrologie*, 4e Aufl. Freiburg 1888. G. Krüger, *Gesch. der altchristl. Litteratur in den ersten drei Jahrh.* Freiburg 1895.
>
> The following can serve as reference aids: for ecclesiastical Greek, the dictionary of J. C. Suicerus, *Thesaurus ecclesiasticus, ed. 2a longe auctior* Amst. 1728, 2 tomi; for medieval Greek, C. Dufresne, dominus du Cange, *Glossarium ad scriptores mediae et infimae graecitatis.* Lugd, 1688, 2 tomi; for medieval Latin, C. Dufresne, dominus du Cange, *Glossarium ad scriptores mediae et infimae latinitatis.* Par. 1678, 3 vol.; a new edition by the Benedictines in 6 volumes, 1733-36; by G. A. L. Henschel, Par. 1840-50 in 7 volumes; and by Leopold Favre in 10 volumes, 1883-87. Also Forcellini, *Totius latinitatis lexicon*, in 4 volumes, Padua 1771; a new edition by Furlanetto in 4 volumes. Padua 1828-’31; an English edition at London 1826; a German edition by Voigtlander and Hertel at Zwickau and Schneeberg 1829-33; a new edition in Italy by Corradini at Padua 1859 and by D. Vinc. De Vit in 6 volumes at Prato 1858-79. For place-names: J. V. Muller, *Lexicon manuale, geographiam antiquam et mediam cum Latine tum Germanice illustrans*, Lips. 1831. J. G. Th. Graesse, *Orbis latinus over Verzeichniss der lateinischen Benennungen der bekanntesten Städte usw.* Dresden 1861.
