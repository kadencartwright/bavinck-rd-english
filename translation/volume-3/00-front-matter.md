Kampen — J. H. Bos — 1898

## Preface

_In working through the extensive material, I have found it impossible to keep the promise I made in the preface to the first volume. This “Reformed Dogmatics” will be complete not in three but in four volumes. The fourth volume will be of the same size as the preceding volumes and will probably appear in about a year. I hope that this expansion will not disappoint the subscribers and readers of this work._

B.
